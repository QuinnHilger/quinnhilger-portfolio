# My Journey Into Software Engineering

I've been thinking alot lately about how I got to where I am today. Its been quite a ride.

## The Beginning

I first started coding in high school. It was just a simple class where we learned basic Java and I remember being so confused by what a "for loop" even was. But something clicked eventually.

The moment I got my first program to work - just a simple calculator - I felt like I had superpowers. I could make the computer do things! That feeling never really went away.

## College Years

UCLA was intense. The CS program there really pushed me. There were times I wanted to quit honestly. Some of those algorithm classes were brutal.

But I also met some amazing people and worked on cool projects. The fraternity helped me develop soft skills that I didnt realize would be so important in the real world.

### Key Moments

- Staying up until 4am to finish my first real project
- Getting my first internship at Pacific Defense
- Realizing that "real" code at companies is often messier than school projects
- Learning that communication skills matter just as much as technical skills

## What I've Learned

Looking back, here are the biggest lessons:

1. **Imposter syndrome is normal** - everyone feels it, even senior engineers
2. **Its okay to not know everything** - thats literally impossible
3. **Ask questions early** - saves so much time and headache
4. **Side projects are invaluable** - SpotMe taught me more than any class

## Whats Next

I'm excited about whats ahead. AI is changing everything and I want to be part of that wave. Working at DraftKings has given me great enterprise experience but I'm always looking for the next challenge.

If your reading this and just starting out - stick with it. It gets easier. Well, different anyway.
